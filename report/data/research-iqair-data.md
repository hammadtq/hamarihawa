WHAT IQAIR ACTUALLY PUBLISHES ONLINE
- IQAir's historical ranking page (https://www.iqair.com/world-most-polluted-cities) exposes month-by-month PM2.5 ONLY for calendar 2025 (expand the ">" chevron next to the 2025 column; filter City = Islamabad to see cities outside the top 50). 2024 and 2026 monthly values are NOT on the site; only annual 2017-2025 values. The 2024/2025 report PDFs are behind a lead form (the guessed /dl/ PDF URLs 404).
- So Sep-Dec 2024 and Jan-Aug 2026 come from a second ground-sensor source, aqi.in (its own sensor network, US-AQI standard, "Annual Air Quality Insights" table on the city historical-analysis pages). aqi.in values differ materially from IQAir for the same months (fewer sensors), so treat them as a different network, not as IQAir.

DEAD ENDS (so you don't repeat them)
- WAQI/aqicn quarterly CSVs need an email magic-link ticket (login) — not possible without creating an account. Lahore/Islamabad US mission stations are in their city list.
- dosairnowdata.org (US State Dept archive) does not resolve DNS anymore; airnow.gov embassy page 404s.
- IQAir report PDFs not fetchable without the form.
- The GitHub "Devixo" Pakistan monthly dataset (MalaikaJabeen repo) is synthetic/modelled: its 2025 Lahore annual is 107.4 and Islamabad 43.7, and Pakistan 2025 = 80.1, all contradicting IQAir. Do not use.
- Kaggle "Lahore Air Quality Index" dataset: not checked in detail (AQI+temperature only per description).
