=== E. ASSESSMENT ===
- Lahore: exactly one model-based projection exists, the World Bank PCAP figure (Lahore Division 87 today -> 99 µg/m³ BAU 2030; 97.5 with program; -6 by 2035). It is a World Bank internal projection flagged as provisional. The Punjab Clean Air Policy's "-30% vs 2022 by 2030 for Lahore" is a target with no modeling behind it. The only other Lahore-specific "projection" is an ARIMA extrapolation to 2025 (A7) and a qualitative CMIP6 stagnation study (A8).
- Islamabad: no projection of any kind (no model, no numeric target). Closest proxies are the AQLI 2023 value and national-level NCAP/CPF targets.
- Regional: the strongest quantitative anchors are the GAINS-based "Punjab (Pakistan)" 2030 rows in Striving for Clean Air (48.8 baseline; 41.9 / 28.6 / 18.9 / 17.3 by scenario) and the PCAP Punjab 52 -> 58 BAU 2030.
- Long-run direction: all climate-meteorology studies (A8-A11 except SSP1) point to more winter stagnation over the IGP; all trend data (AQLI, GBD) show Pakistan worsening through 2023, with a single good 2025 season attributed partly to weather.
- Caveats: I could not open the full text of Mir et al. 2022 or Slater et al. 2024 (paywalled/blocked); their numbers come from abstracts as quoted on SEI/IIASA/search pages. IQAir city-year series for Lahore 2023-24 and Islamabad 2020-25 were not retrievable.
