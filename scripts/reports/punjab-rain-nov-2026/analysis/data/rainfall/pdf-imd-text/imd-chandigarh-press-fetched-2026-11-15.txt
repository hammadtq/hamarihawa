Monthly Outlook for the Rainfall and the Temperatures over Punjab & Haryana during
November 2026
1. Probabilistic Forecast for the Rainfall during November 2026
Below-normal rainfall is likely over most parts of the country except for some areas in central, northwest, east and
Northeast India, where normal to above-normal rainfall is likely during November 2026. The dotted areas shown in the
map receive very less rainfall during November as per climatology. There is no forecast signal from the model over the
white-shaded areas within the land region.
The forecast (Fig.1.) indicates that the below-normal rainfall is likely over most parts of Punjab & Haryana, except
some areas of the region, where normal rainfall is likely.

2. Probabilistic Forecast for the Temperatures during November 2026
Fig.2a and Fig.2b show a probabilistic forecast of the maximum and minimum temperatures, respectively,
during November 2026.
During November, above-normal maximum temperatures are likely over most parts of the country
including Punjab & Haryana (Fig. 2a)..
During November, above normal minimum temperatures are likely over most parts of the country
including Punjab & Haryana (Fig. 2b).
