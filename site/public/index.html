On the map

Pollution sources

Known sources from satellite surveys and mapping. They show where pollution comes from, not how much each one adds to your air today.

How we handle gaps

Fires: shown exactly as NASA detected them, never guessed. Where clouds blocked the view we draw the clouds, so an empty area doesn't always mean no fire.

Pollution layers: we show the newest day that has finished processing, never a half-processed one. Strips the satellite missed are filled from up to two earlier days, and we name those days. Anything still missing stays blank.

City numbers come from a model that covers every city every hour, so they have no gaps, but they are estimates.

Estimates from a pollution model and published research averages, not counts or medical advice. How to read these numbers

City numbers come from the Copernicus (CAMS) air model via Open-Meteo, about 11 km resolution. In winter it reads lower than ground sensors in Lahore and Islamabad by about 1.5×, so the real picture is probably worse.

How we calculate the health numbers
  • Cigarettes: breathing 22 µg/m³ of PM2.5 for a day is roughly like smoking one cigarette (Berkeley Earth).
  • WHO limit: the 24-hour guideline for PM2.5 is 15 µg/m³ (WHO 2021).
  • Years of life: each 1 µg/m³ of PM2.5 above 5, sustained for a year, cuts life expectancy by about 0.098 years (AQLI, University of Chicago).
  • Extra deaths: daily deaths rise about 0.68% for every 10 µg/m³ of PM2.5 above 15 (Liu et al., NEJM 2019). We apply that to each city's normal daily deaths (population × death rate ÷ 365). These are estimates, not counts.

What you can do today

Wear the right mask

Cloth and surgical masks do little. An N95 or KN95 that seals around the nose filters most PM2.5.

Close windows on bad days

When AQI is over 150, keep windows shut, especially early morning and after sunset when smoke settles.

Protect children and elders

Avoid outdoor sport and long walks when the air is red or purple. Asthma and heart patients should keep medicine close.

Report a burning field

Pakistan: call the EPA Punjab helpline 1373 or use the Green Punjab app. India: file a complaint in CPCB's SAMEER app.

Data

  • City air: Copernicus CAMS via Open-Meteo
  • Place names: GeoNames (CC-BY 4.0)
  • Methane and CO₂ plumes: Carbon Mapper (satellite and airborne)
